# 👥 Customer Relationship Management (CRM)

The **Customer Module** manages the database of external clients and partners, tracking their contact details, transaction history, and overall relationship status.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 👤 Profile Management
- **Master Data**: Detailed records of customer demographics, billing, and shipping info.
- **Credit Limits**: Support for customer-specific credit terms and balance tracking.
- **Engagement Logs**: View all sales, invoices, and payments associated with each customer.

### 🛠️ Technical Overview
- **Routing**: `customer.routes.ts` handles the customer list and individual profiles.
- **Service**: `CustomerService` provides the interface for CRM data operations.
