# 📄 Invoice & Billing

The **Invoice Module** handles the creation, distribution, and tracking of customer and supplier invoices.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🧾 Invoice Life-cycle
- **Drafting & Generation**: Create professional invoices with custom branding.
- **PDF Export**: Generate downloadable and printable invoice copies.
- **Status Tracking**: Monitor Overdue, Paid, and Partially Paid invoices.

### 📊 Analytics
- **Billing History**: Detailed logs of all historical billing events.
- **Revenue Insights**: Visual breakdown of top-performing accounts.

### 🛠️ Technical Overview
- **Routing**: Lazy-loaded via `invoice.routes.ts`.
- **Services**: `InvoiceService` manages server-side invoice generation.
